Google Calendar by hand

If the button doesn't work (Google Calendar can't add calendars on a phone, it has to be done on a computer):

  1. Open calendar.google.com on a computer.
  2. On the left, next to Other calendars, click + and choose From URL.
  3. Paste the calendar address from the box above and click Add calendar.

The calendar then shows up in the phone app too (if not, turn it on in the app's sync settings). Google refreshes subscribed calendars every few hours to a day.

One-off import

For a copy that never changes, download saints-cz-en.ics and in Google Calendar choose Settings → Import & export → Import.
